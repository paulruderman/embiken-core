# Terminal

## Terminal is a P.O.S., not a website
Terminal is a restaurant P.O.S., not a website. Large buttons, few screens, one-finger tapping on a fixed counter screen or a tablet held in the other hand. Do not copy Book, Filament, or marketing-web layout. Do not require two-handed or hover use. Training is allowed; extra taps and tiny hit targets are not.

## One-finger tap, other hand free
Layout and hit targets must work with one finger while the other hand holds a tablet, or on a fixed counter screen. Do not require two-handed use, hover, or pinch-zoom to operate.
