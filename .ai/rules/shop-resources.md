# Shop Resources

## Shop Filament Devices and Displays
Shop Filament includes Manager-only Devices and Displays management. Devices are tenant-bound, use one of Terminal, Customer Display, or Wall Display, and pair with short-lived one-time codes. A Customer Display references its paired Terminal; Wall Display Devices may share a Display. Displays select only registered core or enabled-module Feeds and hold the shared standby state.
